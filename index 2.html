Channel Intro
The voice of Umbra Locke
◈ Cold Open — Black Screen
[Silence. A single breath.]
There is a world beneath the one you think you know.
[beat]
Not hidden underground. Not locked behind closed doors. Not buried in classified files or whispered in secret rooms.
[beat]
It's right in front of you.
It has always been right in front of you.
◈ The Figure Appears
[Slow fade in: the suit. No face. The halo.]
The island off the coast of a city of eight million people — where over a million of them were buried, quietly, without a single headstone.
The building in your city with floors that don't exist on any elevator panel.
The law that lets the government take everything you own — without charging you with a crime.
[beat]
None of it is a secret.
All of it is simply... unasked.
◈ The Declaration
[The halo brightens.]
My name is Umbra Locke.
And this is Echo Black.
[beat]
We are not here to frighten you.
We are not here to convince you of anything.
[pause]
We are here because curiosity is the rarest form of courage.
And because once you see something —
[beat]
— you cannot unsee it.
[Title card: ECHO BLACK burns in. Gold on void.]
Begin.
◈ Short Version — Reels / Shorts

◈ Delivery Notes
• Pace: Slower than feels comfortable. The pauses are load-bearing.
• Tone: Not ominous. Certain. Umbra Locke knows, and is choosing to share.
• The figure: Perfectly still until the title card. Stillness is authority.
• Sound: No music until title card. Consider a 40Hz tone — felt more than heard.
• The halo: Sacred, not supernatural. Like ordination, not horror.
